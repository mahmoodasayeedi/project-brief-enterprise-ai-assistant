📊 How we evaluated it

Three layers of evaluation

🔍 1 · Retrieval
Did search open the right document, page, and sentence? Hit rate, recall, MRR and context recall (are the key evidence sentences really in the text the model read?).
✅ Used: finds where a failure starts
📏 2 · BLEU & ROUGE-L
Count words shared with a reference answer. Designed for translation (BLEU) and summaries (ROUGE). They can't tell a wrong answer from a correctly rephrased one.
⚠️ Kept as a baseline only
🧑‍⚖️ 3 · LLM-as-a-judge
A stronger model grades each answer with a rubric: correctness, faithfulness (no hallucination), relevance, and the right behaviour (flag conflicts, warn about old data, refuse off-topic).
✅ Main metric
